Logo anatomy

The mark is two overlapping circles — orange (left) representing warmth and Nepali identity, indigo (right, 88% opacity) representing Australia's trust and community. The blend in the overlap symbolises the two cultures meeting.
The wordmark is Inter 600 — Nep in saffron, Saathi ("friend" in Nepali) in deep purple.
